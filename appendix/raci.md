# Appendix · RACI

**Purpose:** For each recurring engagement decision, name who does the work, who owns the outcome, who gets a say before it lands, and who hears about it after. Most engagement friction is an unnamed accountable person; this grid names them.

**When it is written:** At kickoff, with the sponsor in the room. Revised when staffing changes or when a decision keeps landing on the wrong desk, which is a RACI defect, not a people defect.

## The letters

- **R, Responsible:** does the work.
- **A, Accountable:** owns the decision. Exactly one A per row. Two accountable people means none.
- **C, Consulted:** asked before the decision; their input can change it.
- **I, Informed:** told after the decision; their input cannot change it, and pretending otherwise is how trust erodes.

## Grid (sample rows, fictional engagement)

| Decision | Sponsor | Engagement lead | Design role | Build lead | Client team |
|----------|---------|-----------------|-------------|------------|-------------|
| Approve 01 requirements | A | R | C | C | I |
| Rule on an open decision (OD) | A | R | C | C | I |
| Approve design direction in 04 | A | C | R | I | I |
| Change the schema in 05 | I | A | I | R | I |
| Accept a Phase exit (06) | A | R | C | C | I |
| Call a rollback at launch | I | A | I | R | I |
| Close a risk in the register | A | R | C | C | I |

Rules of the grid:

- One A per row, always. If a row seems to need two, the decision is actually two decisions; split the row.
- The sponsor is Accountable for scope and rulings. The engagement lead is Accountable for the integrity of the set: names consistent, rulings reconciled, decisions logged.
- A Consulted person who is never actually consulted gets moved to Informed at the next revision. The grid describes the engagement as it runs, not as anyone wishes it ran.
