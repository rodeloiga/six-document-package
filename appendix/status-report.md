# Appendix · Status Report

**Purpose:** The weekly account of the engagement, in a fixed shape, so a reader can compare week to week and find what moved, what is next, and what needs a decision. Fixed shape is the point: a status report that changes format weekly cannot be skimmed by anyone busy.

**When it is written:** Weekly, same day, by the engagement lead. It reports the state of the six documents and the plan in 06, not effort expended.

## Skeleton

### Period and milestone state
[Week of [date]. Current phase from 06, and its state against its exit criteria: on track, at risk (name the criterion), or blocked (name the blocker). One paragraph, plain words.]

### Done since last report
[Finished things only, each tied to a document or phase: "03 state inventory completed for all routes" rather than "worked on flows."]

### Next before the next report
[The committed list. Short enough to be checkable next week.]

### Decisions needed
[Gathered from the decision points list in 06 section 5: the decision, its owner, the date it is needed by, and what stalls without it. This section is the nag, and it is allowed to nag.]

### Risks moved
[Risks from the register that changed state, by ID: new, triggered, closed, or escalated, with one line on what changed.]

### Blockers
[Anything the engagement cannot move itself, named with who can. An empty Blockers section says "None," out loud.]
